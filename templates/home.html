What is Sports Arbitrage?

Sports arbitrage (surebets) is a risk-free betting strategy that takes advantage of pricing discrepancies between different bookmakers. By placing opposing bets on all possible outcomes of an event with different bookmakers, you can guarantee a profit regardless of the outcome.
